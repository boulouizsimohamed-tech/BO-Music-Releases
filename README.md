# 🎵 BO Music

**BO Music** is a sleek, minimalist, high-contrast **offline local music and video player** for Android, crafted with Flutter for a smooth, distraction-free media experience.

## ✨ Key Features

- **100% Offline Playback:** Play all your local audio and video files directly from your device with zero internet connection required.
- **Custom Color Themes & Floating Selector:** Personalize the interface on the fly using an intuitive floating theme switcher with Light, Dark, and custom accent palettes.
- **Collapsible Mini-Player:** Browse your library seamlessly while keeping full playback controls accessible in a smooth, collapsible mini-player.
- **Custom Video Sizing Overlays:** Switch effortlessly between fit, fill, and custom aspect-ratio overlays while watching local videos.
- **Earphone Media Button Controls:** Full hardware media button support (play, pause, next, previous) directly from wired or Bluetooth earphones.
- **Scheduled Daily Notifications:** Smart, customizable daily reminders to jump back into your favorite playlists.
- **In-App Auto-Updates:** Automatically checks for new official releases and lets you update the app directly with one tap.

## 📥 How to Download & Install (Android)

1. Go to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/boulouizsimohamed-tech/BO-Music-Releases/releases/latest)** page (or click the **Download APK** badge above).
2. Under the **Assets** section, tap on **`BO-Music-v1.1.0.apk`** to download it to your Android device.
3. Open the downloaded `.apk` file from your notifications or File Manager.
4. If prompted by Android, enable **"Allow from this source"** (Install Unknown Apps) and tap **Install**.
